MVP formula (ensemble):
  score = z(P_logistic) + z(share_ridge) + z(raw VORP)
Each term is standardized within the season being scored (mean 0, std 1),
then added. Logistic and Ridge use the same within-season percentile-ranked
feature set as the logistic / pct variants; VORP is the raw Basketball Reference value.
Highest ensemble score in a season = predicted MVP.
